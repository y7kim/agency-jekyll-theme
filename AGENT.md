# AMGCUBE Website Codex Instructions

## Project goal

This is the company website for AMGCUBE Pty Ltd.

The website is mainly a trust-building company profile site for potential clients who have already contacted AMGCUBE. It is not primarily a cold lead-generation website.

## Company positioning

AMGCUBE is a Brisbane-based company focused on granny flat design, prefabricated building systems, project coordination, and local delivery.

AMGCUBE's own builder licence is currently under application. Do not state that AMGCUBE is currently a licensed builder.

Completed display home and early projects were delivered in collaboration with a licensed Queensland building partner.

## Wording rules

Do not use:
- AMGCUBE is a licensed builder
- licensed builder
- builder licence pending
- QBCC licence under application
- fully licensed builder
- we build granny flats

Use instead:
- AMGCUBE provides granny flat solutions
- prefabricated building systems
- project coordination
- building work is carried out by licensed Queensland builders and qualified trades where required
- delivered in collaboration with a licensed Queensland building partner

## Tone

Professional, simple, factual, trustworthy.
Avoid exaggerated marketing language.
Avoid over-promising on price, time, approval, or compliance.

## Website priorities

1. Improve trust.
2. Clarify delivery model.
3. Strengthen the Oxley display home as proof.
4. Fix spelling and inconsistent wording.
5. Keep the existing website structure and design unless necessary.

## Verification

After each change:
- Show changed files.
- Summarize what changed.
- Mention any missing information, such as ABN, construction partner name, QBCC licence number, or email address.
