 ### Flexible finishes for personalized living
 AMGCUBE HOMES offers a wide range of finishing options across exterior façades, interior walls, and flooring systems.<br>
 From paint and timber to stone and tile, homeowners have the flexibility to create a space that reflects their personal style while maintaining consistency in quality and performance.<br>

 #### Exterior finishes
 A variety of exterior wall finishes are available, including painted surfaces and decorative cladding systems.<br>
 Materials such as panels, tiles, or stone veneers can be applied to achieve different architectural styles and visual effects.<br>

 #### Interior wall finishes
 Interior walls support a wide selection of finish materials, including wallpaper, wood veneer, and stone.<br>
 These finishes are prefabricated where possible and installed onto prepared wall panels, improving efficiency and finish quality.<br>

 #### Flooring options
 Multiple flooring solutions are available, including timber flooring, tiles, carpet, and epoxy finishes.<br>
 Each option is selected to balance durability, aesthetics, and ease of installation within the overall building system.
