Planning & Feasibility

Every site is different. Before moving into design or construction, we help assess the key factors that affect whether and how a granny flat can be built on your land.

These may include zoning, overlays, setbacks, site access, slope, existing structures, and local approval requirements. Our goal is to give you a clearer understanding of what is possible before you commit too far.

  • Initial site and project feasibility review
  • Guidance on likely approval pathway
  • Advice on access, layout, and design constraints
02

Cost & Timeline

One of the biggest concerns for homeowners is uncertainty. We aim to reduce that by using a more systemised building approach, with clearer scopes, more predictable timelines, and fewer unknowns than a purely traditional build process.

Project cost depends on size, layout, finishes, and site conditions. Timeline depends on approval requirements, site preparation, and construction scope. A typical project can often be completed in around 3 to 4 months from construction commencement to final completion.

  • Clearer scope before construction starts
  • Reduced on-site build time through prefabrication
  • Better visibility on delivery milestones
03

Why Build with AMGCUBE

AMGCUBE combines the efficiency of prefabrication with the flexibility of on-site construction. Rather than relying on fully modular building pods, our system uses prefabricated components that are assembled onsite, making it easier to adapt to real site conditions while maintaining speed and consistency.

This approach allows better control over quality, construction sequencing, and finish integration, while also creating more flexibility in design, layout, and material selection.

Behind AMGCUBE’s work in Australia is a technology foundation that has already been tested and applied in large-scale real-world projects, giving us confidence in the practicality and reliability of the system.

  • Faster and more controlled construction process
  • Flexible layouts and finish options
  • Technology supported by real project experience
04

Compliance & Delivery

All projects must comply with Australian building requirements, and this remains a core part of our delivery approach. We work within the local approval and construction framework, using licensed professionals where required and completing inspections through the appropriate process.

From design support and approval coordination through to component delivery, on-site construction, final inspection, and certification, we focus on turning a complex project into a more manageable and transparent experience for the homeowner.

  • Built in line with Australian standards and regulations
  • Licensed trades and required inspections
  • Support from planning through to completion
