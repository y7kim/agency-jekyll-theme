What we believe

We believe housing should be faster, smarter, and more accessible.

This future is not far away. And we are here to build it.

STORY

  • OCTOBER 2023

    We Began

    In response to Australia's housing crisis, we took the first step toward change.

  • OCTOBER 2024

    Technical Prototype Completed

    We partnered with MODCUBE, a leading prefabrication company in China, to develop our first housing prototype for the Australian market.

  • JANUARY 2025

    AMGCUBE Launched

    AMGCUBE HOMES was officially introduced, bringing a new approach to residential construction in Australia.

  • JUNE 2025

    Display House construction started

    Construction of our first display home began, testing our system in real local conditions.

  • JANUARY 2026

    Official Launch to Market

    Our first display home was completed and fully approved. We officially begin delivering high-quality granny flats to homeowners across Brisbane.
