AMGCUBE HOMES

AMGCUBE HOMES is a prefabricated housing solution, combining a heavy-duty steel frame, advanced envelope systems, and modular interiors to achieve high product quality, rapid delivery, and cost-efficient manufacturing.

Each AMGCUBE unit is a precision-engineered module, factory-built and pre-fitted with essential components—frame, walls, kitchen, bathroom, electrical and plumbing systems, and interior finishes—delivered fully assembled and move-in ready.

With off-site prefabrication, AMGCUBE requires only basic foundations and on-site assembly—dramatically reducing construction time and labor costs while enhancing quality control and sustainability.

AMGCUBE can serve as a standalone home, granny flat, or be scaled into townhouses, eco resorts, workforce housing, student accommodation, and more.

It’s not just a building—it’s a reimagined product for living spaces.
