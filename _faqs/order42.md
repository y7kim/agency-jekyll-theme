  #### Can I hire my own contractor for installation?
  Installation of AMGCBUE homes requires specialized experience and equipment.<br> While we strongly recommend using our certified installation team, you may engage your own builder provided they obtain prior approval from AMGCBUE’s project management. All external contractors must meet AMGCBUE’s quality, safety, and scheduling standards.
