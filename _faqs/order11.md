title: Product vs. Project：What’s the difference for a homeowner?
category: order
order: 11
answer: | 
  #### The fundamental difference lies in **Certainty.**<br><br>
  With traditional construction, cost, schedule, and quality are highly dependent on project management, and they require close coordination of many variables—weather, site conditions, labor availability, material supply, and so forth. In the Australian context, all of these variables introduce significant uncertainty.<br><br>
  By contrast, a prefabricated modular housing “product” is designed, finalized, and then produced under controlled factory conditions. Once design is complete, cost, timeline, and quality are effectively locked in. The only remaining factors that could delay final delivery are truly systemic, force-majeure events.
