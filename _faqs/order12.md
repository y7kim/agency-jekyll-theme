 #### How is the quality of a AMGCBUE home? 
 Although AMGCBUE is delivered as a prefabricated product, all the building materials used are among the mainstream, high-quality materials found in Australian homes today. <br><br>
 #### Does it feel comfortable to live in?
 In terms of overall construction quality and living experience, a AMGCBUE home is fully on par with—or even superior to—a conventionally built house, especially in areas such as insulation, soundproofing, and wall strength.
