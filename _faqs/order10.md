title: AMGCBUE Homes VS traditional home
category: order
order: 10
answer: | 
   #### From a regulatory standpoint, there is essentially no difference. <br><br>
   AMGCBUE follows all of the same steps as conventional construction—professional architectural design, project approval and permitting, on-site assembly, and final inspection by government authorities—each step must comply with Australia’s building codes and relevant oversight requirements.<br><br>

   #### However, in terms of construction methodology, the two approaches diverge significantly. <br><br>
   AMGCBUE is delivered as a prefabricated, modular building product, whereas conventional construction operates as a bespoke building project.
