answer: | 
  #### 1. **Consultation**  
   We begin with mutual consultation. Once you and AMGCBUE agree on the overall concept and feasibility, both parties sign a nonbinding service-intention agreement. <br><br> 
  #### 2. **Design**  
   AMGCBUE’s design team develops the architectural and engineering plans. After you approve the final design, both parties sign the formal construction contract.<br><br>  
  #### 3. **Permitting**  
   AMGCBUE handles all necessary submissions to local authorities and obtains the building permit. Once approval is secured, the project moves to the next phase. <br><br> 
  #### 4. **Production, Transport & Assembly**  
   Following the production schedule, AMGCBUE manufactures the modules in our factory, transports them to your site, and completes on-site assembly. Once assembly is finished, the home is ready for handover.  <br><br>
  #### 5. **Final Inspection & Maintenance**  
   AMGCBUE organizes the final inspection with relevant authorities and provides warranty and maintenance services as specified in the contract.
